Orbit Tower

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2025

Summary

Orbit Tower is a casual one-touch arcade stacking game. It does not ask you to create an account. The app shows third-party ads. Your high score and other small game data stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.

This policy applies to Orbit Tower on Android and iOS. The same rules cover both versions of the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or photos through the app.

What we do not collect directly. We do not operate a login and we do not receive your gameplay data on our servers.

Advertising. The app displays ads from third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob). To show, personalize, measure, and prevent fraud related to ads, those partners may process technical and usage data such as:

  • advertising ID (for example Google Advertising ID or Apple’s ID for advertising)
  • device type, operating system, and app version
  • IP address (often used to infer general region)
  • ad impressions, clicks, and interactions with ads

That processing is done by the ad partners under their own privacy policies, not by us as the developer collecting a profile on our servers. We do not use your on-device high score for advertising.

Data stored on your device

Game-related data is stored only on your device. On Android the app uses SharedPreferences. On iOS the same information stays in the app’s local storage. We do not operate a backend, and this data is not uploaded to our servers.

The app may store the following on your device:

  • your highest score (high score record)
  • privacy policy acceptance version and time (if you accept the in-app policy)

How we use information

On-device data is used only to run the game: for example to remember your high score between sessions and whether you accepted the privacy policy. We do not use that on-device gameplay data for advertising, analytics, or profiling, and we do not receive a copy of it.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your on-device gameplay data with third parties. Because that data stays on your device, we do not receive a copy to share.

When ads are shown, third-party advertising partners may collect or receive the information described in the Advertising section above, in line with their own policies. They are independent from us.

If you install Orbit Tower from Google Play, Google may process store-related information (such as your Google account and install activity) under its own rules. See the Google Privacy Policy.

If you install it from the App Store, Apple may process store-related information under its own rules. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect a child’s name, email address, or other contact details through the app. Orbit Tower can be played without giving us those details. Ads may still be served by third-party partners; their handling of data is described in their own policies and in the device settings below.

Security

Game data remains on your device and is not sent to our servers. It is protected by your device’s own security, including the app sandbox and any screen lock you use. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage in system settings, the locally stored game data is removed as part of that action.

Your choices

  • Uninstall or clear data. You can uninstall Orbit Tower from your device, or clear the app’s storage in Android or iOS settings. That removes locally stored data such as your high score, according to the system’s normal behavior.
  • Ads on your device. On Android you can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings. On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. Options depend on your OS version.

International users

The app does not transmit gameplay data to us, so we do not transfer that data across borders. If you download the app from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple may process store-related data in other countries, as described in their privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Please review this page occasionally. The date of the current version is November 15, 2025.
